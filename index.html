EVALUATION BENCHMARKS

Datasets & Metrics

Evaluated across three complementary inertial navigation benchmarks spanning single-device and multi-device wearable setups.

ATE (m) Absolute Trajectory Error — root-mean-square 2D positional deviation against ground truth over the full trajectory.
RTE-60s (m) Relative Trajectory Error over 60-second windows — measures local velocity & heading consistency.
PRE (%) Path Relative Error — percentage ratio of trajectory error to total path length traversed.
